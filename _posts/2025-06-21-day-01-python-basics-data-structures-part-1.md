## 📅 Day 1: June 21, 2025

### 📝 Phase 1: Python Basics (Review)

Today, I started my 30-day training plan by focusing on the absolute fundamentals of Python: **Data Structures**.

This is a review, but it's essential. Data structures are the "containers" that hold information, and you can't build anything in AI without them. I'm focusing on the two most common ones today.

### 🧠 Concepts Reviewed

* **Lists:**
    * **What they are:** The most common container. A simple, ordered, and *changeable* collection of items.
    * **Analogy:** A shopping list where you can add, remove, or change items in order.
    * **Code:** `my_list = [1, "hello", 3.0]`

* **Dictionaries:**
    * **What they are:** A collection of **key-value** pairs. They are extremely fast for looking up information.
    * **Analogy:** A real-world dictionary. The "key" is the word (e.g., "Apple"), and the "value" is the definition (e.g., "A red fruit").
    * **Code:** `my_dict = {"name": "Ritik", "skill": "Python"}`

### ✅ Today's Goal
The goal was to refresh my memory on how to store and access data. This is crucial for Phase 2 when I start learning about libraries like Pandas, which are built on these ideas.
